AI has turned up nearly everywhere this year. A restaurant is a strange place for it, though. Eating out runs on people: someone reads the room, notices you're in a hurry, tells you the special is actually worth it tonight. That part is hard to hand over.

So Max went to see Tim Yu, who founded Zubu Ramen in 2020, and asked him straight out. Would a robot ever replace the servers? Would it replace the chefs?
