Vol. 3 ran at [Wren Café](https://www.bravoup.ca/store/wren-cafe) in Yaletown on August 8.

The subject was the one this series keeps circling: AI moving out of the chat window and into the ordinary business of eating out. Finding somewhere. Booking it. Ordering, paying, and getting something back for it.

![Guests talking around cafe tables during the session](images/p1.webp)

The difference on the night was that people used it rather than heard about it. Bravo AI is the assistant that does those steps for you, across a network of more than 500 restaurants. The room tried it on their own phones instead of watching a slide about it.

The demo happened at the counter, not from a stage. That felt about right for an evening about ordinary commerce. Afterwards people stayed at the tables and worked through the app together, which is the part you can't stage.

![The Wren Cafe room filled with attendees seated at tables](images/p2.webp)

![A speaker demonstrating Bravo AI to attendees at the counter](images/p3.webp)

Wren Café hosted and fed everyone. That is not a small thing to ask of a café that also has to run a normal day.

![Attendees trying the app together after the talk](images/p4.webp "aspect: 980/1070; focus: 50% 100%")

From prompt to plate, for real. See you at the next one.
